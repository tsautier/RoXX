ONE POLICY POINT

Your network access, under one roof.

Through FreeRADIUS, RoXX connects RADIUS clients to identity sources, adds multi-factor checks, and gives operators a web console for configuration, users, logs, and service health.

ARCHITECTURE

CAPABILITIES

Built for real access workflows.

RADIUS integration

03

Multi-factor access
